Snooze Fiend Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 22, 2026

The short version

Snooze Fiend collects no data. Everything the app knows about you stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

Your alarms, pomodoro settings, game progress (experience, coins, stage, cosmetics), your chosen display name, and any friend codes you paste are stored locally on your device only. The app has no servers, no analytics, no advertising, and no network features. Nothing is transmitted anywhere.

Friend codes

The Crew feature works by exchanging short text codes between friends directly (for example over a messaging app of your choice). A code contains only the display name and game statistics the sender chose to share. The app never sends or receives these codes itself.

Permissions

The app requests alarm and notification permissions solely to ring alarms you set and to show the alarm screen. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal data.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version ever changes any of the above, this policy will be updated before that version ships.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Mozetech/snooze-fiend/issues.
